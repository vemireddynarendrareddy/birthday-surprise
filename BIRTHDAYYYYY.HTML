❤️ Chapter 1 ❤️

The Moment My Eyes Found You 🌹

Every beautiful story has a beginning...

Some moments in life are not planned...

They just happen...

And later... we realize that one small moment changed everything. ❤️

It was during my Intermediate 2nd year public exams...

A normal day... A normal place...

But for me, that day became a memory I could never forget. ✨

I still remember that beautiful moment in the function hall...

When I was going into the function hall and opened the gate, I saw you standing straight there...

For a few seconds... everything around me felt silent. ❤️

My eyes stopped there... and my heart remembered that moment.

I didn't know why that moment felt so special...

But somewhere inside me, I felt there was something different about you.

At that time, I never knew...

That the person I saw for the first time would become the most special person in my life.

That was not just a first look...

It was the first page of our beautiful story. ❤️

A small moment... A beautiful memory... A beginning of forever. ✨

❤️ Chapter 2 ❤️

The Day Destiny Introduced Us ✨

Life has its own beautiful way of connecting two people...

Sometimes, the most unexpected moments become our favourite memories. ❤️

During my first year of B.Tech,

I visited Annamacharya College, Kadapa, for my project presentation.

I never expected that a normal college visit would become one of the most memorable days of my life. ✨

Because that was the day...

I met you. ❤️

I still remember that little moment...

I didn't shake hands with you, and I never knew that small thing made you feel a little sad.

If I could go back to that moment...

I would definitely hold your hand...

Because that small missed moment became the first page of our beautiful journey. ❤️

And yes...

That was also the day we captured our first picture together. 📸

A simple picture...

But a forever memory. ❤️

That day was not just a meeting...

It was destiny quietly writing the next chapter of our story. ✨
```
```

❤️ Chapter 3 ❤️

The Bond That Started Growing 🌹

After that day, our bond slowly started growing.

Long distance was never easy for us...

Sometimes, it took months just for us to meet again.

There were many struggles, many difficult moments, and many situations where we had to be patient.

But through everything, our understanding became stronger. ❤️

We slowly learned how to understand each other, care for each other, and stay together even when distance tried to make things difficult.

And honestly...

The moment I see your chubby face and that beautiful smile, I forget everything else. ❤️

Your smile, your beard, and the way you care for me...

Everything about you became so special to me.

You love me so much, and you always take such good care of me.

Somewhere along the way, you stopped being just a person in my life...

You became my comfort, my happiness, and someone I never want to lose. ❤️

❤️ Chapter 4 ❤️

My Forever Person ❤️

Somewhere between all our conversations, memories, fights, happiness, and the time we spent together...

I slowly realized how deeply I had fallen for you. ❤️

You became someone I could never imagine my life without.

I know I have hurt you many times...

I know there were moments when I made you feel bad, and moments when our fights became too much.

But even after all those moments...

you still stayed.

You still loved me.

You still cared for me.

And that made me understand how much love you actually have for me. ❤️

You are not perfect... and neither am I.

But somehow, our imperfect story feels perfect to me.

I don't just love you for the happy moments...

I love you for staying through the difficult ones too.

You became my favourite person, my safest place, and the person I want beside me for a long, long time. ❤️
